## Decisions (each is also a Ruling in the ledger)

- **P1 Panels carry no energy.** The Electrical branch stays under the Site (R3) but each panel is mapped only to `voltage_v` and `current_a` (current follows the feeds it carries, I = P / (sqrt(3) x V x 0.95)). Reason: facts 3 and 5. The docs say so and say that a panel's kWh needs a "downstream of" relation (a later wave).
- **P2 Free and single-feed racks.** A free rack keeps its Feed A and Feed B assets, with no mapping and no readings (its energy is "no data"). A single-feed rack has only Feed A (carrying the whole load).
- **P3 Numbering.** Racks are numbered across a hall (Hall 1: Rack 01-59, Hall 2: Rack 01-58) and rows across a hall (Row 1-4, two per pod), so a name is unique inside its hall and a quick-find hit is rarely ambiguous. Hall 2 repeats the numbers of Hall 1 (different parents).
- **P4 Where the `~` lives.** The 8 power-only feeds are the B feeds of 8 live racks of Hall 2, so Hall 1 is exact and Hall 2, the Site and the totals show `~`. The one gap feed and the one reset counter are Feed A of two live racks of Hall 1.
- **P5 Kinds.** `choose_kinds(registered_keys)`: per role the first preferred kind that is registered, else "Other equipment" if registered, else the first preferred name unregistered, flagged (warning in the dry run and the report). Preferred: site "Site"; hall "Data hall", "Room"; pod "Room"; row "Row"; rack "Rack"; feed "Circuit", "Meter"; electrical group "Room"; panel "Panel". Nothing is ever registered.
- **P6 Interval.** `--interval-minutes` in {5, 10, 15, 30, 60} (`history.INTERVAL_CHOICES`, re-exported by `pilot_hall`; the mapping's `interval_seconds` equals the real spacing); the default `DEFAULT_INTERVAL_MINUTES` is **60 until Task 5 measures** (review finding 1: the old test file says a rollup refresh costs "a few milliseconds per reading", and 233 meters give about 162k readings for 29 days at 60 minutes, 650k at 15, 1.95M at 5, i.e. roughly 8 min, 30 min and 1.5 h of refresh at that cost); the tests use 60.
- **P7 Memory and flushes.** Readings are generated per UTC day (counter state persists across days), one batch per UTC day through `history.write_batches` (no slicing: the largest day, 233 meters x 288 samples = 67,104 rows at 5 minutes, is under `Writer(max_buffer=100_000)`; a test pins `len(layout.meters) * 288 < 90_000`).
- **P8 Progress and timing.** The write phase prints one stdout line every 5 days ("written 5 of 29 days"); the final report prints the seconds spent writing and refreshing; the dry run says that a run takes minutes and that `--days N` or a larger `--interval-minutes` makes it faster.
- **P10 Module boundary.** `pilot_hall.py` does `from dcdash.seed import history` and calls `history._now()`, `history.make_plan`, `history.pending_drop`, `history.refresh_rollups`, `history.write_batches` and `history._undo` through the module (the tests monkeypatch those names on `history`); every `except history.Refused` for this layout lives inside `pilot_hall` (`python -m dcdash.seed.history` runs history as `__main__`, so `pilot_hall` imports a second copy of it). `history._parser` never imports `pilot_hall` at module top (`INTERVAL_CHOICES` is defined in `history`).
- **P11 What half-way means.** Before `create_rows` commits nothing is written. After it, any exception or Ctrl+C (`except BaseException`, as `history._seed`) runs `history._undo` with this layout's undo sentence and removes the source, the assets, the points, the mappings, the readings and re-refreshes the rollups. A killed process leaves a seed that the next run refuses; remove it with the normal deletes. `docker compose exec` Ctrl+C stops the client, NOT the process in the container: a second run started at once is refused while the first still writes (the docs say so).
- **P9 The tree.** Site "Seeded pilot hall" (sort 0) > "Hall 1", "Hall 2" > "Pod A", "Pod B" > "Row n" (rack counts per row, in order across both halls: 15, 15, 14, 15 | 15, 15, 13, 15 = 117) > "Rack nn" > "Feed A"/"Feed B"; and "Electrical" (sort_order 1, so the halls list first) > "Panel 1A", "Panel 1B" ... "Panel 4B" (pod number across the site 1-4, letter = feed). Variety (counts fixed, which racks varies with `--seed`): 6 free racks, 6 idle racks (0.15-0.45 kW), 5 single-feed racks, the rest live (2.0-11.0 kW), A share 0.5 +- 0.05, 8 power-only feeds, 1 gap, 1 reset.

### Task 3: The series generator
