## 1. Facts checked in the code (each line of the brief re-read; corrections marked FIX)
- `month_costs` (`backend/dcdash/api/billing.py:91`) and the energy engine (`core/energy.py`, `core/cost.py`) are read as the brief says. The engine reads `readings_1h` (a real-time continuous aggregate of `readings_1m`; columns `point_id, bucket, min_value, max_value, sum_value, n, last_value, minutes`; bad-quality samples excluded; a row exists only for an hour with at least one good sample). `_ROWS` returns the month's rows, each COUNTER point's baseline (the last bucket before `start`) and every point's first bucket ever.
- `counter_hours`: previous = baseline or None; `last < previous` is a reset (`max(0, max-prev) + (last-min)`); otherwise `last - previous` (so an hour whose `min < previous <= last` is a dip the engine treats as a plain rise); no previous: `last - min`. `power_hours`: rows with `n <= 0` skipped; coverage `min(1, minutes/60)` when `interval_seconds <= 60` (`MINUTE_COVERAGE_MAX_INTERVAL`), else `min(1, n*interval/3600)`; hour = `sum/n * coverage`. A power-only meter with no rows is `HourEnergy(0, True, has_data=False)` for every hour of the range; a silent counter is `{}`. Splice: an own meter counts from `own_from = max(first_bucket, start)`; before that the asset is the sum of its children (`assemble`). `HourEnergy.estimated = not meter.counter`. `cost_by_hour` prices an own hour with `rate_at(tariffs, tree, asset, bucket.astimezone(zone).date())`; `_price`: `unpriced = kwh > 0 and rate is None` (kwh already scaled).
- `load_meters` picks per asset the `energy_kwh` mapping, else `active_power_kw` (`Meter(point_id, scale, interval_seconds, counter)`). Voltage/current-only assets (the seeded panels) have NO meter.
- `AssetTree` has `children`, `ancestors_or_self`, `path`, `preorder`, `nodes`. `billing._now()` is the clock the billing tests patch (`monkeypatch.setattr("dcdash.api.billing._now", ...)`); the new endpoint must call it through the module so one patch moves both endpoints.

## 2. Decisions (Rulings; the plan review may overturn them)
- **D1 Read-only; nothing existing moves.** The diff touches NONE of `core/energy.py`, `core/cost.py`, `core/billing_close.py`, `api/billing.py` (a final `git diff --stat main -- <those>` must print nothing). Proof of R1: that diff, the existing billing suites untouched and green, and a `month_costs` JSON for the finished seeded month saved on the pre-change look stack (Task 0) and compared byte for byte after the last backend commit (the finished month's readings do not change).
- **D2 Where the maths lives.** `backend/dcdash/core/month_check.py` (pure, no database, imports `HourRow`, `Meter`, `counter_hours`, `power_hours`, `MINUTE_COVERAGE_MAX_INTERVAL` from `energy.py`; edits nothing there). The route and the one batched statement live in a new `backend/dcdash/api/billing_check.py` (router registered after `billing.router`; same 409 words as `month_costs` for an unusable zone, pinned by a test that compares the two texts).
- **D3 What is looked at.** Every asset in `load_meters` (its OWN meter; parents are not repeated), over the UTC hours that begin in `[month start, stop)` where `stop = min(month end, floor_to_hour(now))` (an open month stops at the last COMPLETE hour). A meter's expected hours start at `max(first_bucket, month start)`; a point that NEVER recorded anything (`first_bucket` None) has EVERY hour of `[start, stop)` as expected hours and counts in `meters` / `meter_hours` (review C1); a meter whose first reading is at or after `stop` (after the month, or in the hour still running) has none and is NOT counted anywhere except the information group `later` (review I6). State: `not_begun` (now before the month; nothing is read), `open`, `finished` (by the site clock, like the page's `month < current`). `hours` = hours in `[start, stop)` (0 when the first hour of the month has not finished: "nothing to look at yet").
- **D4 The seven findings and the rules (the contract of section 3).** Per meter, in order of how much they can change a bill:
  1. `silent`: `never` (the point never recorded anything; `hours` = its expected hours, `since` and `last_reading` null), `none_in_month` (it has earlier readings but none in the expected range; `hours` = its expected hours, `since` null, `last_reading` = the last bucket before the month or null), `trailing` (the last expected hours have no reading: `since` = first hour of the trailing run, `hours` = its length, `last_reading` = the bucket of the last row in the range).
  2. `gaps`: runs of whole expected hours with no row, EXCEPT the trailing run (that is `silent`). Counted: `runs`, `hours`, `longest_hours` with `longest_start`, `first_start` (start of the first missing hour), `last_end` (end of the last missing hour); `leading_hours` = length of the run that begins at the month's first hour (0 if none) and `previous_reading` = the bucket of the reading before the month a COUNTER's first row after such a hole is measured from (the baseline; null for power-only or none) (review I7). **Counter**: a run no longer than `tolerated` is normal and ignored, where `tolerated = 0` for polling under 3600 s and `min(24, ceil(interval/3600))` hours for 3600 s and slower (a slow poll leaves hourly buckets empty by design; the same tolerance applies to the trailing run; capped at 24 h because `interval_seconds` has no upper bound, review M1). **Power-only**: no tolerance (the engine counts an hour without a row as nothing), and also `short_hours` / `short_minutes` = hours that HAVE a row but are covered less than fully by the engine's own coverage rule (`row_coverage`, identical to the factor in `power_hours`). The item exists when there is a run OR the short time is worth listing: `short_minutes >= max(60, expected_minutes / 100)` (review I8: a 60 s poll with 0.2 s of read time drifts about 144 minutes a month and must not list every meter; a meter losing a whole minute every hour is 1.7 percent and is listed); below that threshold `short_hours` and `short_minutes` are reported as 0. A counter's part-covered hour loses no energy inside the month (the hour counts last minus previous last), so it is never reported (at the month's last hour up to 59 minutes of energy can move into the next month: said in the docs, review M2).
  3. `estimated`: every power-only meter WITH expected hours (listed even when silent, because the page shows `~` for it; a power-only meter whose first reading is after `stop` is in `later`, the page shows no `~` for it): `hours_estimated` = hours with a row and `n > 0` (exactly the hours `power_hours` returns), `hours_total` = its expected hours.
  4. `resets` (counters): rows classified as `counter_hours` branches: `reset` when `last < previous`, `dip` when `min < previous <= last`, and `fell` (review I10a: a counter never goes down, so `max > last` proves a reading went down inside the hour) when neither of those and `max > last`; the first row with no previous is never `reset` or `dip` but can be `fell`; the baseline is the last bucket before the month (as in the engine). Returned: the full counts `resets`, `dips`, `falls` and the first five `events` by time. A `dip` and a `fell` leave the engine's figure as `last - previous` (the glitch is ignored), a `reset` makes that hour approximate: the wording says which (review M3). Not detectable and said so in the docs: a reset that climbs back past the earlier reading inside one hour with `max == last`, a first hour that starts from a reading of 0, a huge torn read as the last sample of the last hour (review I10 b and c, parked in backlog BE: they need a size threshold).
  5. `started`: `month start < first_bucket < stop` (the asset counts the sum of its children before it, nothing if it has none); `children` = number of children. Counted as a thing to check. (`first_bucket >= stop` is NOT here: see `later` below.)
  6. `unpriced`: own hours of the meter with scaled `kwh > 0` and no rate (rate lookup by local date of the hour, `rate_at`), summed: `kwh`, `hours`, `days`, `first_day`, `last_day`.
  7. `flat` (review C2 and I11): a run of consecutive ROWS (a missing hour between two rows does NOT break it: the value before and after the hole is the same) whose counted energy is exactly zero by the engine's own rule: for a COUNTER a row where `counter_hours` gives 0 on the plain branch (`last == previous`, or for the first row with no baseline `last == min`; a reset row is never flat; a dip with `last == previous` stays flat); for a POWER-ONLY meter a row with `min == max` equal to the value of the run's first row. Its length is the clock time from the first flat row's bucket to the end of the last flat row's hour; it is a finding at `FLAT_HOURS = 24` hours or more. Returned: `runs` (runs of that length), `longest_hours`, `longest_start`, `first_start`. A row with energy, or a reset, ends a run.
  8. `unmetered` (information only, never counted, the brief's R4(g)): leaf assets (no children) that have no meter of their own and no meter above them (nothing measures them). `total` and the first 50. Each item has `beside_measured` (review I9): the leaf's PARENT has at least one meter somewhere beneath it, i.e. the asset above is billed from other meters under it and this one's power is not in that figure; those items sort FIRST. Pilot-hall expectation: 20 items, 0 beside measured.
  9. `later` (information only, never counted): meters whose first reading is at or after `stop`.
- **D5 What counts as a "thing".** `things` = the sum of the `total` of the seven groups 1-7 (one per meter and kind of finding). `unmetered` and `later` are never counted. `meters` = meters with at least one expected hour (so a `never` meter counts, a `later` one does not); `meter_hours` = the sum of their expected hours.
- **D6 Wording is on the client, from structured data**, so the vitest suite can scan every sentence for banned words (WHOLE words, case-insensitive, `\b(ok|ready|verified|correct|accurate|guarantee|safe|all good|passed)\b`, plus a `%` sign: "Looked at" must not trip it, review M4). Headline, decided in this order (review C1): `things > 0` gives `N things to check`; else `state == not_begun` or `hours == 0` gives `Nothing to look at yet` (with its reason); else `meters == 0` gives `Nothing to look at yet` (no meter recorded in this month); else `Nothing to check`. A unit test pins each branch, including "every meter is `never`" (gives `N things to check`). Every answer says what it looked at ("Looked at 217 meters over 720 hours (150,480 hours of readings)", from `meters` and `meter_hours`, never "720 hours each" because a meter may have started later). The owner's estimate rule is ONE sentence in the Estimated group: "An hour is marked estimated when the asset has no energy (kWh) counter, so its energy is worked out from average power (kW) readings; anything that adds up estimated hours is marked estimated too." Counter versus power consequences: counter gap = the energy is not lost, it is counted in the hour the readings return (in a later month when the gap runs past month end); power-only gap = that time counts as 0 kWh.
- **D7 Times** in the site timezone as `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM` (new `formatSiteDateHour` in `lib/siteTime.ts`); the API returns ISO UTC and the client formats.
- **D8 Thresholds (stated in the answer under `limits` and in the docs):** `flat_hours = 24`, `short_minutes = 60` together with `short_share = 0.01` (a power-only meter's partly-covered time is listed only when it is at least 60 minutes AND one hundredth of its expected minutes), `max_tolerated_hours = 24` (the counter polling tolerance cap), `cap = 50` items per group (the answer carries `total`, so "and N more" is always the true number).

## 3. The API contract (the front end and the tests are written against exactly this)
`GET /api/billing/month-check?month=YYYY-MM` (gate `billing.view`; `month` optional = the current site month; pattern `^\d{4}-(0[1-9]|1[0-2])$` as `/costs`; 422/409 as `/costs`).
```
{ "month": "2026-09", "timezone": "Asia/Qatar", "state": "not_begun"|"open"|"finished", "closed": false,
  "from": "2026-08-31T21:00:00Z", "to": "2026-09-30T21:00:00Z", "month_end": "2026-09-30T21:00:00Z",
  "hours": 720, "meters": 217, "meter_hours": 150480, "things": 12,
  "limits": {"flat_hours": 24, "short_minutes": 60, "short_share": 0.01, "max_tolerated_hours": 24, "cap": 50},
  "groups": { "silent": G, "gaps": G, "estimated": G, "resets": G, "started": G, "unpriced": G, "flat": G,
              "unmetered": {"total": 20, "items": [{"asset_id": 1, "path": "A / B", "beside_measured": false}]},
              "later": {"total": 0, "items": [{"asset_id": 1, "path": "A / B", "kind": "counter", "first_reading": iso}]} } }
G = {"total": int, "items": [Item]}            // items: at most cap, worst first, ties by path then asset_id
Head = {asset_id, path, kind: "counter"|"power", point: str|null, source: str|null, interval_seconds}
silent    = Head + {reason: "never"|"none_in_month"|"trailing", since: iso|null, hours: int, last_reading: iso|null}
gaps      = Head + {runs, hours, longest_hours: int|null, longest_start: iso|null, first_start: iso|null, last_end: iso|null,
                    leading_hours: int, previous_reading: iso|null, short_hours: int, short_minutes: int}   // runs may be 0 for a power-only meter listed for its short time; a counter's short_* are 0
estimated = Head + {hours_estimated, hours_total}
resets    = Head + {resets, dips, falls, events: [{at: iso, kind: "reset"|"dip"|"fell"}]}   // the three are the FULL counts; events are the first five by time
started   = Head + {first_reading: iso, children: int}
unpriced  = Head + {kwh: float, hours: int, days: int, first_day: "YYYY-MM-DD", last_day: "YYYY-MM-DD"}
flat      = Head + {runs, longest_hours, longest_start: iso, first_start: iso}
```
`to` is `stop` (the end of the last complete hour looked at; for a finished month it equals `month_end`); `from` is the month start. For `not_begun`: `from` = the month start, `to` = `from`, `hours` 0, empty groups. `closed` = the month has an active close (its billed figures are frozen; the check describes today's stored readings). Worst first: silent by hours desc (`never` first), gaps by hours desc, estimated by hours_estimated desc, resets by resets+dips+falls desc, started by first_reading desc, unpriced by kwh desc, flat by longest_hours desc, unmetered with `beside_measured` first then by path. `things` counts the seven G groups only. A `not_begun` month, or `hours == 0`, returns empty groups and `things: 0`.
